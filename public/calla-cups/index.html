— Who we are

Calla & Cups is your local.

We've been part of Canterbury since 2019 — pulling shots, plating brunch, and learning everyone's coffee order. We're not trying to be everywhere. We're trying to be the best café on this street.

Our beans come from a Melbourne roaster we've trusted for years. Our bread comes from a baker two streets away. Everything we make has a reason for being here.
