AI almost stole 3 hours of my life last night 😃 
I was working on a small personal project when AI confidently told me I had a ~3-hour engineering problem to solve. It sounded reasonable enough that I immediately had that familiar engineering feeling of, “Well… guess my evening is gone.” For about 30 seconds, I mentally accepted my fate and prepared for the usual debugging rabbit hole.
Then I pushed back a little.
A few questions later, AI replied: “You’re right, and I was wrong.”
Turns out the whole thing was based on a false assumption. No fix. No rewrite. No late-night debugging spiral. Just… not actually a real problem.
One thing I’m slowly realising is that the value of AI is not only faster execution. Sometimes the real value is faster falsification. A few years ago, I probably would have spent half a day proving this wrong manually. Now it sometimes takes minutes to realise the thing never needed to be done in the first place.
Also, mildly satisfying when AI apologises to you.
