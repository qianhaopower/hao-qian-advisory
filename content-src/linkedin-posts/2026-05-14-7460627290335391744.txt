If I could bring all of these tools on my current laptop back to my job seven or eight years ago, a full week of work might only take a few hours now.

Maybe even less.

---

This was my desk last night while working on a personal project.

Multiple windows running at the same time:

coding, 
reviewing outputs, 
thinking through product decisions, 
checking deployment flows.

At the same time, part of my brain was casually watching a video on the other monitor about how to cook pork belly properly.

---

Not because I suddenly became superhuman.

But because many AI tools can now independently carry a reasonably complete piece of work for a while.

I no longer need to manually drive every small step continuously.

More and more, my role becomes:

reviewing direction, 
making trade-offs, 
deciding what should happen next.

---

What feels strange is not just the speed.

It’s how normal it already feels.

---

The world is changing very, very fast.
