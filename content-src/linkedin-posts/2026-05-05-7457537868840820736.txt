We’re closing month-end 5 days faster.

No extra hires.
No new systems.

Four weeks ago, I connected Claude to Xero via an MCP server.

10-minute setup. £5/month custom connector.

Since then, everything’s changed.

Here’s what it’s actually doing at month-end:

• Flags journals, accruals, and issues before I’ve even opened the file
• Analyses variances vs budget and prior periods and tells me what actually matters
• Drafts commentary and variance analysis 
• Builds KPI dashboards and the management pack

And outside of month-end:

• Sends weekly AR chaser updates to clients automatically
• Answers ad-hoc questions and pulls reports without me logging into Xero

Tasks that used to take an afternoon now take 10 minutes.

But it’s not perfect.
It lacks context, It guesses on outliers and It gets things wrong, just like a junior.

The difference?
You train it the same way.
Give it context, Explain why the numbers look the way they do, Teach it what to flag next time and unlike a junior… it doesn’t forget.

AI won’t replace accountants.
But accountants using AI will replace the ones who don’t.
