Some of you may have noticed that I've been experimenting with AI lately.

I've always enjoyed building things, and AI has made it possible to turn ideas into something real much faster.

Here is one small thing I've been working on — a self-assessment app that asks 35 simple questions across 7 wellbeing pillars: finance, relationships, information (what you take in online), emotions, nutrition, dynamic (movement and activity), and sleep.

It takes around 8 minutes and gives you a few personalised daily practice suggestions, such as "praise someone with kind words" or "eat one meal without screens." No sign-up needed to see your results.

Still early. Still learning. But I do hope it's worth a few minutes of your time for a small moment of self-reflection.
