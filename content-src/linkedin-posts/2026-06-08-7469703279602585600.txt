AI said it would not “eyeball it.”
Which is impressive.
Because it does not have eyeballs.
I was debugging a tiny UI alignment issue and said the VS badge looked slightly off. The agent disagreed, then started taking screenshots, writing measurement scripts, calculating pixel offsets, and producing a very serious investigation report.
The whole time it kept acting like it was the responsible adult in the room because it was not going to “eyeball it.”
Mate.
You literally cannot eyeball anything.
The AI era is wild.
Sometimes the funniest part is not that AI makes mistakes.
It is how confidently it borrows human expressions for things it cannot physically do.
