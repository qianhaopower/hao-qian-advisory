Working Theory #20 — The Fitness Bank

A lot of the conversation around exercise is about outcomes.

Lose some weight. 
Look better. 
Perform better.

---

Those are real.

But they’re not the full picture.

---

Fitness works like a bank account.

---

Every workout is a small deposit — a run, a lift, a stretch. None of them feels life-changing, but over time they add up, and they compound.

---

Because your body adapts.

The more you build, 
the more you can handle.

---

A larger fitness balance means:

you can train more, 
recover better, 
and grow further.

---

Just like money.

More savings → more interest → faster growth.

---

And the world is moving faster than ever.

---

Each year you stay healthy, mobile, and energetic,

you give yourself more chances to be part of what’s coming next.

---

Fitness is not just about how you feel today.

It’s about whether you’re still capable when the future arrives.

---

Keep making deposits.
