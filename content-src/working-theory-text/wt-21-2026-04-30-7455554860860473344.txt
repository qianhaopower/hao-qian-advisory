Working Theory #21 — The Relationship Bank

Any meaningful relationship works like a bank account.

---

It doesn’t grow from one big moment.

It grows from small, consistent deposits.

---

A look. 
A question. 
A check-in. 
A hug. 
A small act of care.

---

None of them feels significant on its own.

But over time, they add up.

---

And they build trust.

---

Big gestures are memorable.

But they don’t replace the balance.

---

What sustains a relationship 
is the quiet, repeated signals:

I see you. 
I care. 
I’m here.

---

This is true at home.

And it’s true at work.

---

Strong relationships at work aren’t built in one project.

They’re built over time:

helping each other, 
sharing ideas, 
going through challenges together.

---

That’s what creates real connection.

---

Like any account,

you can build it up.

Or slowly let it run down.

---

Keep showing up. 
Keep taking care of it.
