Working Theory #17 — The Domain Size Theory

Traditionally, we use two signals to judge how senior a leader is:

Title 
Org size

---

How big is the team? 
How many people report to them?

---

That made sense.

Because in the past,

problem size scaled with team size.

---

Bigger problem → more people 
More people → more senior leader

---

That mapping is breaking.

---

With AI,

small teams can now handle much larger domains.

Fewer people, 
but significantly more complexity.

---

You can already see it:

Small startups building products 
that compete with much larger organisations

Leaders owning end-to-end systems 
that used to require multiple teams

---

So the signal is shifting.

---

It’s less about how many people you manage.

It’s more about what you own.

---

Not tasks. Not projects.

Problem domains.

---

For example:

Owning whether an entire product ecosystem stays up or breaks 

Owning how customer needs become real products 

Owning how products connect, evolve, and scale across the company 

---

These are large surfaces of complexity.

---

But there’s a more personal signal.

---

You know your seniority is increasing when:

important problems 
cannot bypass you

---

When your input is needed 
before decisions are made 

When your judgment shapes the direction 

When your absence creates a gap 

---

That’s not about control.

It’s about ownership.

---

Not headcount.

But domain size.
