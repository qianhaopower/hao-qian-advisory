Working Theory #22 — The Trust Dividend

Some relationships reach a different level.

---

You don’t need to explain everything.

You don’t need to add context, 
justify decisions, 
or walk through every step.

---

You just say a few words.

And the other person gets it.

---

Because you’ve been through things together.

Projects that didn’t go well. 
Moments that did. 
Reorgs. 
Tough decisions. 
People coming and going.

---

Over time,

that shared experience builds something deeper.

---

Trust.

---

Not the kind you talk about.

The kind you rely on.

---

You trust that they will make the right call. 
Just like you would.

---

And work starts to flow differently.

Less friction. 
Faster decisions. 
Cleaner execution.

---

This kind of trust can’t be rushed.

It’s built over years.

---

And when you have it,

you feel it.

---

It’s rare.

And worth treasuring.
