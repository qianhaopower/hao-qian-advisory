Working Theory #25 — The Human Signal Premium

In the AI era, human relationships may become more important, not less.

---

Because we are entering a world where it becomes harder and harder to tell what is real.

---

The message you read 
may be AI-generated.

The voice on the phone 
may be AI-generated.

The video you watch 
may not even contain a real person anymore.

---

And honestly, most of the time, we probably won’t be able to tell.

---

As AI becomes better at sounding human, 
looking human, 
and behaving human,

the value of actual human connection increases.

---

Not random interaction.

Real connection.

---

People you truly know.

People with shared history. 
Shared memories. 
Shared experiences.

People you have gone through life with.

---

Because trust in the future may no longer come from polish, intelligence, or presentation.

Those things are becoming cheap.

---

Trust may increasingly come from something much harder to fake:

History.

Consistency.

Presence over time.

---

The people who were there before the AI wave.

The people you know are real.

---

And I think that changes both life and business.

---

In a world full of artificial signals,

real human relationships become premium signals.
