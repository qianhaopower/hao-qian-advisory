Working Theory #19 — The Thinking Multiplier

AI is separating people into two groups:

Those who use it as an amplifier. 
And those who use it as a replacement.

---

One group uses AI to push their thinking.

They question more. 
Explore alternatives. 
Test assumptions. 

The more they use it, 
the stronger their thinking gets.

---

The other group uses AI to avoid thinking.

They ask once. 
Take the answer. 
Move on. 

The more they rely on it, 
the less they build that muscle.

---

You can see it in moments like this.

A team needs to decide what to work on next.

---

One person asks AI for suggestions, 
picks one, and moves forward.

---

Another pulls context from different places, 
uses AI to compare options, 
and pushes on trade-offs.

---

Same tool. Very different outcome.

---

Over time,

one group handles more complexity 
and makes better decisions.

The other becomes faster,

but narrower.

---

AI doesn’t decide which group you’re in.

You do.
