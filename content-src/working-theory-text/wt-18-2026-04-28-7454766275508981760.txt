Working Theory #18 — The Attention Budget

It has never been easier to produce content.

You can write paragraphs in seconds. 
Generate code instantly. 
Send messages without thinking twice.

---

Creating information is cheap.

Reading it isn’t.

---

Every message you send 
takes someone’s time.

Every paragraph you write 
uses someone’s attention.

---

And people don’t have much of it.

---

So the responsibility changes.

---

It’s not just about saying something.

It’s about whether it’s worth saying.

---

Does this sentence carry meaning? 
Does this message serve a purpose? 
Does this code solve something?

---

Or is it just filling space?

---

When we waste someone’s attention,

we don’t just lose clarity.

We lose trust.

---

Over time, people learn:

This person is worth listening to. 
Or not.

---

Respecting attention 
is not about writing less.

It’s about making each piece count.
