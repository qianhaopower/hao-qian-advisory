## Script as written (captions follow what he ACTUALLY says)

Have you ever felt frustrated because your great idea never became a
decision?

You explained it.
You pushed it.
Nothing moved.

When that happens, debug the decision line.

As engineers, we trace stuck traffic through a system:
Where did it stop?
What blocked it?

Managers should debug decisions the same way.
Who needs to align?
Who might block it?
Who has the final say?
Which forum?
Who needs to say yes—and by when?

In one case, I knew three people needed to say yes, and two needed not
to say no.
Before pushing the decision, I spoke to all five and got a clear signal
from each.
When the time came, the decision happened.

If a signal is missing, that is where you focus.
Talk to the right person.
Sharpen the pitch.
Bring the right data.
Find common ground.
Build consensus.

Managers don't just wait for decisions.
They clear the path for decisions to be made.

That is how you turn an idea into a decision—and a decision into
reality.

Suggested punch words (adjust to delivery): NOTHING MOVED / DEBUG /
STUCK / WHO / 3 YES / 2 NOT NO / CLEAR SIGNAL / THE PATH / REALITY
