**Hao's final text (2026-09-15), ~110 words ≈ 55 s:**

The more senior you get, the more relaxed you have to be.

People think that's because you delegate more, or because you've seen it all. No. The
real reason: it's the only way to survive.

The higher you go, the more decisions land on you. Harder ones. Less information, more
at stake.

A stressed brain makes bad calls. That's physiology, not attitude. Under stress, the
part of your brain that weighs options goes silent, and the part that reacts takes
over. You stop weighing. You start reacting.

So when the pressure goes up, that's exactly the moment to slow down.

Calm isn't the reward for being senior. Calm is a must.

## What changed from the draft
- "should feel more relaxed" → "have to be" (a claim, not advice).
- "not only because… also because…" → "People think X. The real reason: Y." (same
  logic, no negation-first construction, sayable in one breath).
- "only when you are relaxed can you make good decisions" → "a stressed brain makes bad
  calls" (drops the inversion he'd fumble).
- "due to how our frontal cortex works" → the mechanism in plain words. The claim is
  solid: stress hormones impair prefrontal function and shift control to reactive
  circuits (Arnsten, Nat Rev Neurosci 2009). No need to cite on camera.
- Added the slot for HIS example. The draft asked the audience for one and gave none.
- Ending lands on the punch line instead of a question.

## Delivery notes for this take
- Grammar traps in this script (his #1 pattern): brain **makes**, decisions **land**,
  the part **goes** quiet / **takes** over.
- Hands out of frame by default. One gesture: "goes quiet" (hand drops), "takes over"
  (hand rises). Then rest.
- The example is the only place a think-pause is allowed. Open and close are pre-formed.
- Cover frame will be frame 0: start speaking on "The more senior".
