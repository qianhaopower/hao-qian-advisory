## Teleprompter script (CAPS = punch words, blank line = pause)

How do you grow a person on your team?

Same way a good trainer grows you.
They add FIVE kilos to the bar.
Maybe ten. Never twenty.
Enough that you feel it.
Light enough that you RECOVER.

Careers work the same way.
The job is to keep finding
the GOLDILOCKS load —
the extra weight that's just right.

In a career, that extra weight
comes in a few shapes:
a harder technical problem,
a RISKIER deployment,
one more team to align,
or a project that runs MONTHS
instead of weeks.

Get the load right, and the person
steps out of their comfort zone,
learns something new —
and still feels neither STRESSED
nor DEFEATED.

Here's one from my team.

One engineer could already coordinate
inside our team,
and with the team next door.

The next level:
aligning teams BEYOND our org.

So I set it up on purpose, as a stretch.
ONE extra team beyond our org.
Just one.

He aligned them beautifully.

And from that point on,
his range grew —
and so did my trust.
Next time something like that comes up,
it's HIS.

So here's my working theory:
people grow at the GOLDILOCKS load.
A little more than they can carry today.
Light enough to RECOVER from.

And finding that weight —
again and again, one person at a time —
that's most of the manager's job.
